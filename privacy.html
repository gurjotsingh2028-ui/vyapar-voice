Vyapar Voice - Privacy Policy

On your device. Your khata entries and app settings (like language) are saved in your browser's storage on your device. This happens whether or not you sign in.

If you sign in. With Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture (Google's basic profile). With an email link, we receive only your email address. Your entries are then synced to our database (hosted by Supabase) so you can use your khata on other devices. Signing in is optional - skip it and your entries stay only on your device.

Voice input. Voice typing uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, which may send the audio to the browser or device provider's speech service to convert it into text. Vyapar Voice receives only the converted text, which becomes your entry. The app itself does not record or store audio.

How data is used. Your name, email and entries are used to provide the khata and sync features. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking code.

Who processes your data. Google (Google sign-in), Supabase (sign-in accounts and synced entries), and your browser or device provider's speech service when you use voice typing. Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data. You can delete any entry inside the app - it is marked deleted, hidden from your khata, and the deletion syncs to your other signed-in devices. Entries stored only on your device can be removed by clearing the app's site data in your browser.
